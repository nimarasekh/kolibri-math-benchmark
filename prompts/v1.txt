Let \(C\) be the one-object category whose endomorphism monoid is
\[
\mathbb N^2=\langle p,q\mid pq=qp\rangle.
\]

Let
\[
A=(\mathbb Z/24)^4,
\]
and let
\[
F:C\to\mathcal S
\]
be the functor with
\[
F(*)=K(A,3),
\]
such that \(F(p)\) and \(F(q)\) are induced by the automorphisms
\[
\phi(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)
=
(x_1+4x_2,\;x_2,\;x_3+3x_4,\;x_4)
\]
and
\[
\psi(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)
=
(x_1+6x_2,\;x_2,\;x_3+8x_4,\;x_4),
\]
respectively.

Let
\[
L_F\longrightarrow N(C)
\]
be the left fibration classified by \(F\), and let
\[
\Gamma(N(C),L_F)
\]
denote its space of sections.

What is the order of the finite abelian group
\[
\pi_1\Gamma(N(C),L_F)?
\]

Give your final answer as a single integer. Your response must contain nothing else; any additional text will be counted as incorrect.
