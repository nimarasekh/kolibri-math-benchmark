Let C = N([2]), and let

F : [2] -> S

take values in discrete spaces with

F(0) = {a,b,c},
F(1) = {1,...,7},
F(2) = {x,y,z,w}.

Suppose the map F(0) -> F(1) sends a,b,c to 1,2,3, respectively, and F(1) -> F(2) sends

1,2 -> x,
3,4 -> y,
5 -> z,
6,7 -> w.

Let L_F -> C be the left fibration classified by F, and let L_1 -> C be the left fibration classified by the covariant representable

Map_C(1,-).

How many connected components does

Map_{LFib(C)}(L_1,L_F)

have?

Give your final answer as a single integer. Your response must contain nothing else; any additional text will be counted as incorrect.
