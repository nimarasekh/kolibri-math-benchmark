\[
\mathrm{Fin}^{\simeq}
\]
denote the symmetric monoidal \(\infty\)-groupoid of finite sets and bijections, with monoidal structure given by disjoint union.

\[
X\simeq \coprod_{n\ge 0} B\Sigma_n.
\]

Let
\[
X^{\mathrm{gp}}
\]
denote its group completion as an \(E_\infty\)-space, and let
\[
(X^{\mathrm{gp}})_0
\]
be the connected component containing the unit.

What is the order of the finite group
\[
\pi_7\bigl((X^{\mathrm{gp}})_0\bigr)?
\]
