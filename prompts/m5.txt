Let C be the category with objects

x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4,y_1,y_2,y_3,y_4,y_5,

with exactly one nonidentity morphism

x_i -> y_j

for every 1 <= i <= 4 and 1 <= j <= 5, and no other nonidentity morphisms.

Let C^gpd denote its infinity-groupoid completion.

What is the rank of
pi_1(C^gpd, x_1)?

Give your final answer as a single integer. Your response must contain nothing else; any additional text will be counted as incorrect.
