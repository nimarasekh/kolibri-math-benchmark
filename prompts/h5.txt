Let

f : X -> Y
and
g : U -> V

be maps of finite discrete spaces. Suppose Y has three elements and the three fibers of f have cardinalities

1, 2, 3,

while V has three elements and the three fibers of g have cardinalities

1, 2, 3.

Regard f and g as objects of

Fun(Delta^1, S).

How many connected components does

Map_{Fun(Delta^1,S)}(f,g)

have?

Give your final answer as a single integer. Your response must contain nothing else; any additional text will be counted as incorrect.
