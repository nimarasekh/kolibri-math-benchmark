Let \(C\) be the category
\[
b\longleftarrow a\longrightarrow c,
\]
and let \(D=C^{\triangleright}\) be obtained by adjoining a terminal object \(t\). Let
\[
j:C\hookrightarrow D.
\]

Define \(F:C\to\mathcal S\) by
\[
F(a)=F(b)=F(c)=T^2=S^1\times S^1.
\]

With respect to the standard bases of \(H_1(T^2;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^2\), suppose that the two structure maps induce the matrices
\[
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&2\\
6&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
N=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&2\\
6&2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Let
\[
X=(\operatorname{Lan}_jF)(t).
\]

What is the order of the torsion subgroup of
\[
H_1(X;\mathbb Z)?
\]

Give your final answer as a single integer. Your response must contain nothing else; any additional text will be counted as incorrect.
