Let \(\mathcal B\) be the poset, regarded as a category, of all nonzero proper linear subspaces
\[
0\neq V\subsetneq \mathbb F_3^4,
\]
ordered by inclusion.

Let
\[
N(\mathcal B)
\]
be its nerve.

What is the rank of the abelian group
\[
\widetilde H_2\bigl(|N(\mathcal B)|;\mathbb Z\bigr)?
\]

Give your final answer as a single integer. Your response must contain nothing else; any additional text will be counted as incorrect.
