Let C be the category with objects 0,1 and precisely three nonidentity arrows

alpha,beta,gamma : 0 -> 1.

Let p : E -> N(C) be the Cartesian fibration classified by a functor

F : C^op -> Cat_infinity

taking values in discrete categories, with

F(1) = {1,...,10},
F(0) = {a,b,c,d}.

The three reindexing functors F(1) -> F(0) are given, on 1,...,10, by

alpha^* = (a,a,b,b,c,c,d,d,a,b),
beta^*  = (a,b,b,b,c,d,d,d,a,c),
gamma^* = (a,a,b,c,c,c,d,a,a,b).

How many equivalence classes of Cartesian sections of p are there?
