Let \(\mathcal I\) be the ordinary category with objects
\[
x_0,x_1,y_0,y_1
\]
and exactly four nonidentity morphisms
\[
x_i\longrightarrow y_j,
\qquad i,j\in\{0,1\}.
\]

Let
\[
\mathcal C=\mathcal I\times\mathcal I,
\]
and consider the stable \(\infty\)-category
\[
\operatorname{Fun}\bigl(N(\mathcal C),\operatorname{Mod}_{H\mathbb Z}\bigr).
\]

Let
\[
\Delta:\operatorname{Mod}_{H\mathbb Z}
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Fun}\bigl(N(\mathcal C),\operatorname{Mod}_{H\mathbb Z}\bigr)
\]
denote the constant-diagram functor, and set
\[
F=\Delta H(\mathbb Z/12),
\qquad
G=\Delta\Sigma^2 H(\mathbb Z/18).
\]

What is the cardinality of
\[
\pi_0\operatorname{Map}_{\operatorname{Fun}(N(\mathcal C),\operatorname{Mod}_{H\mathbb Z})}
(F,G)?
\]

Give your final answer as a single integer. Your response must contain nothing else; any additional text will be counted as incorrect.
