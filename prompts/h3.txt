Let S denote the infinity-category of spaces.

Let C be the category with four objects a,b,c,d, with precisely the four nonidentity arrows

a -> c,
a -> d,
b -> c,
b -> d.

Let D be obtained from C by freely adjoining a terminal object t, and let

j : C -> D

be the inclusion.

Let

F : C -> S

be the constant functor with value S^1, with every morphism acting by the identity.

Let Lan_j F : D -> S denote the left Kan extension computed in the infinity-category of spaces (so the relevant colimits are infinity-categorical colimits).

What is the rank of

H_1((Lan_j F)(t); Z)?
