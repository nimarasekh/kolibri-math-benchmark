Let \(C\) be the category
\[
a\longrightarrow c\longleftarrow b,
\]
and let \(D=C^{\triangleleft}\) be obtained by adjoining an initial object \(t\). Let
\[
j:C\hookrightarrow D.
\]

Define \(F:C\to\mathcal S\) by
\[
F(a)=K(\mathbb Z/12\oplus\mathbb Z/18,2),
\]
\[
F(b)=K(\mathbb Z/8,2),
\]
\[
F(c)=K(\mathbb Z/24,2).
\]

The map \(F(a)\to F(c)\) is induced on \(\pi_2\) by
\[
(x,y)\longmapsto 2x+4y\pmod{24},
\]
and the map \(F(b)\to F(c)\) is induced on \(\pi_2\) by
\[
z\longmapsto 3z\pmod{24}.
\]

Let
\[
P=(\operatorname{Ran}_jF)(t).
\]

What is the order of the finite group
\[
\pi_2(P)?
\]

Give your final answer as a single integer. Your response must contain nothing else; any additional text will be counted as incorrect.
