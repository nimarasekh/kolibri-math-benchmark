Let
\[
C=B\mathbb Z^2
\]
be the one-object \(\infty\)-groupoid with commuting generators \(p,q\).

Define a functor
\[
F:C\longrightarrow \Cat_\infty
\]
by
\[
F(*)=B\mathbb Z^4.
\]
The actions of \(p\) and \(q\) on \(\mathbb Z^4\) are given respectively by the commuting automorphisms
\[
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
-2&1&-1&4\\
1&-1&2&-3\\
0&0&1&0\\
0&0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}
\]
and
\[
N=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&1&-2\\
0&1&-6&5\\
0&0&-4&3\\
0&0&1&-1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Let
\[
\mathcal E=\int_C F
\]
be the \(\infty\)-categorical Grothendieck construction, equivalently the total \(\infty\)-category of the coCartesian fibration over \(C\) classified by \(F\).

Let
\[
\mathcal E^{\mathrm{gpd}}
\]
denote its \(\infty\)-groupoid completion.

What is the order of the torsion subgroup of
\[
H_1(\mathcal E^{\mathrm{gpd}};\mathbb Z)?
\]
