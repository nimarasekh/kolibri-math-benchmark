Let
\[
\mathcal X=\operatorname{Shv}(T^2;\mathcal S)
\]
be the \(\infty\)-topos of sheaves of spaces on the torus.

Let
\[
A=(\mathbb Z/12)^2,
\]
and let \(\mathcal A\) be the locally constant sheaf of abelian groups on \(T^2\) whose monodromies around the two standard generators of
\[
\pi_1(T^2)\cong \mathbb Z^2
\]
are the commuting automorphisms
\[
M(x,y)=(x+2y,y),
\qquad
N(x,y)=(x+3y,y).
\]

Let
\[
K(\mathcal A,1)\in\mathcal X
\]
be the Eilenberg--Mac Lane object associated to \(\mathcal A\), and let \(1_{\mathcal X}\) denote the terminal object.

What is the cardinality of
\[
\pi_0\operatorname{Map}_{\mathcal X}
\bigl(1_{\mathcal X},K(\mathcal A,1)\bigr)?
\]

Give your final answer as a single integer. Your response must contain nothing else; any additional text will be counted as incorrect.
