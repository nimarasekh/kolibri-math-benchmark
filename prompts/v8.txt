\[
\operatorname{Mod}_{H\mathbb Z}.

\[
f:
H(\mathbb Z/12\oplus\mathbb Z/20)

\[
(x,y)\longmapsto(3x,3y).

C=\operatorname{cofib}(f).

\[
\pi_0
\operatorname{Map}_{\operatorname{Mod}_{H\mathbb Z}}
