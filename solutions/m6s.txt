Answer: 11

Explanation:
Connected components of

Map(BV_4,BS_4)

correspond to conjugacy classes of homomorphisms V_4 -> S_4.

There is 1 class with trivial image.

For image of order 2, the kernel can be any of the 3 subgroups of order 2 in V_4. In S_4 there are 2 conjugacy classes of elements of order 2: transpositions and double transpositions. Hence this gives

3*2 = 6

classes.

For injective homomorphisms, S_4 has two conjugacy types of V_4-subgroups. The normal Klein four subgroup of double transpositions gives 1 conjugacy class of embeddings, while a subgroup generated by two disjoint transpositions gives 3 classes of embeddings.

Thus the total is

1 + 6 + 1 + 3 = 11.

Final answer: 11
