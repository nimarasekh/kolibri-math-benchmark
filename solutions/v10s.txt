Let
\[
A=K(\mathbb Z^2,2),\qquad
B=K(\mathbb Z^3,2).
\]
Since \(X\) is the homotopy cofiber of
\[
f:A\to B,
\]
representability by Eilenberg--Mac Lane spaces gives
\[
\pi_0\operatorname{Map}_*(X,K(\mathbb Z/12,6))
\cong
\widetilde H^6(X;\mathbb Z/12).
\]

The cofiber sequence gives the long exact cohomology sequence
\[
H^5(A;\mathbb Z/12)
\longrightarrow
\widetilde H^6(X;\mathbb Z/12)
\longrightarrow
H^6(B;\mathbb Z/12)
\xrightarrow{f^*}
H^6(A;\mathbb Z/12).
\]
Since \(K(\mathbb Z^r,2)\simeq(\mathbb CP^\infty)^r\) has cohomology only
in even degrees,
\[
H^5(A;\mathbb Z/12)=0.
\]
Therefore
\[
\widetilde H^6(X;\mathbb Z/12)
\cong
\ker(f^*:H^6(B;\mathbb Z/12)\to H^6(A;\mathbb Z/12)).
\]

Write
\[
H^*(B;\mathbb Z/12)
\cong
(\mathbb Z/12)[x_1,x_2,x_3],
\]
and
\[
H^*(A;\mathbb Z/12)
\cong
(\mathbb Z/12)[u,v],
\]
with all generators in degree \(2\). The map on degree-two classes is
\[
x_1\mapsto2u,\qquad
x_2\mapsto3v,\qquad
x_3\mapsto2u+3v.
\]

Degree six corresponds to homogeneous cubic polynomials. Thus the source
is \((\mathbb Z/12)^{10}\), with basis the ten degree-three monomials in
\(x_1,x_2,x_3\), while the target is \((\mathbb Z/12)^4\), with basis
\[
u^3,\quad u^2v,\quad uv^2,\quad v^3.
\]

Over the integers, the substitution map is represented by
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
8&0&0&0&8&0&0&8&0&8\\
36&12&0&0&24&12&0&12&12&0\\
54&36&18&0&18&18&18&0&0&0\\
27&27&27&27&0&0&0&0&0&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its Smith normal form over \(\mathbb Z\) has nonzero diagonal entries
\[
1,\;6,\;36,\;216.
\]

Reducing modulo \(12\), the image therefore has cardinality
\[
\frac{12}{\gcd(12,1)}
\cdot
\frac{12}{\gcd(12,6)}
\cdot
\frac{12}{\gcd(12,36)}
\cdot
\frac{12}{\gcd(12,216)}
=
12\cdot2\cdot1\cdot1
=
24.
\]

The source has cardinality
\[
12^{10}=61917364224.
\]
Hence the kernel has cardinality
\[
\frac{12^{10}}{24}
=
2579890176.
\]

Therefore the mapping space has exactly
\[
2579890176
\]
connected components.
