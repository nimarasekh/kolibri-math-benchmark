Answer: 8

Explanation:
For any space Y and abelian group A,

pi_0 Map(Y,K(A,n))
~= H^n(Y;A).

Therefore

pi_0 Map(
  K(Z^2,2),
  K(Z/2,4)
)

is

H^4(K(Z^2,2); Z/2).

Since

K(Z^2,2)
~= K(Z,2) x K(Z,2)
~= CP^infinity x CP^infinity,

its mod-2 cohomology ring is

H^*(K(Z^2,2);Z/2)
~= (Z/2)[x_1,x_2],

where x_1 and x_2 both have degree 2.

The degree-4 part has basis

x_1^2, x_1 x_2, x_2^2.

Hence

H^4(K(Z^2,2);Z/2)
~= (Z/2)^3.

This group has

2^3 = 8

elements. Therefore the mapping space has 8 connected components.

Final answer: 8
