Answer: 13

Explanation:
In the presheaf infinity-category

P(C) = Fun(C^op,S),

the Yoneda lemma gives

Map(y(a),F) ~= F(a),
Map(y(b),F) ~= F(b),
Map(y(c),F) ~= F(c).

Since

X = y(a) amalgamated_{y(c)} y(b)

is a pushout, mapping out of X turns this pushout into a pullback:

Map(X,F)
~=
F(a) x_{F(c)} F(b).

All spaces involved are discrete, so the number of connected components is simply the cardinality of this pullback.

For the four elements of F(c), the fiber sizes of

F(a) -> F(c)

are

3,1,2,2,

while the fiber sizes of

F(b) -> F(c)

are

1,4,2,1.

The pullback cardinality is therefore the sum of the products of corresponding fiber sizes:

3*1 + 1*4 + 2*2 + 2*1
= 3 + 4 + 4 + 2
= 13.

Final answer: 13
