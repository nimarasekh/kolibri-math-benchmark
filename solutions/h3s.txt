Answer: 2

Explanation:
The pointwise formula for a left Kan extension gives

(Lan_j F)(t) ~= colim_{(j/t)} F.

Since t is freely adjoined as a terminal object, the comma category (j/t) is equivalent to C itself. Hence

(Lan_j F)(t) ~= colim_C F.

The functor F is constant at S^1 and every structure map is the identity. For a constant diagram with value X in spaces, its homotopy colimit over C is

X x |N(C)|.

Thus

(Lan_j F)(t) ~= S^1 x |N(C)|.

The nerve of C has four vertices a,b,c,d and four nonidentity arrows

a->c, a->d, b->c, b->d.

There are no nondegenerate simplices of dimension greater than 1, so |N(C)| is the graph K_{2,2}. This graph is a single cycle, hence

|N(C)| ~= S^1.

Therefore

(Lan_j F)(t) ~= S^1 x S^1,

the 2-torus. Its first integral homology is

H_1(S^1 x S^1; Z) ~= Z^2.

The rank is therefore 2.

Final answer: 2
