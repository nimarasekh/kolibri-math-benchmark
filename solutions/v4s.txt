Since \(t\) is the adjoined initial object, the pointwise formula for the
right Kan extension identifies
\[
P=(\operatorname{Ran}_jF)(t)
\]
with the homotopy limit of the cospan
\[
F(a)\longrightarrow F(c)\longleftarrow F(b).
\]
Thus
\[
P\simeq
K(\mathbb Z/12\oplus\mathbb Z/18,2)
\times^h_{K(\mathbb Z/24,2)}
K(\mathbb Z/8,2).
\]

The homotopy long exact sequence for this homotopy pullback gives
\[
\pi_2(P)
\cong
\ker\left(
(\mathbb Z/12\oplus\mathbb Z/18)\oplus\mathbb Z/8
\longrightarrow
\mathbb Z/24
\right),
\]
where
\[
(x,y,z)\longmapsto 2x+4y-3z.
\]

The image is all of \(\mathbb Z/24\), because \(2,4,3\) generate the
unit ideal modulo \(24\):
\[
\gcd(24,2,4,3)=1.
\]
Hence the map is surjective.

The domain has order
\[
12\cdot18\cdot8=1728.
\]
Therefore
\[
|\pi_2(P)|
=
\frac{1728}{24}
=
72.
