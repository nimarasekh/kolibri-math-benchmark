Answer: 729

The poset \(\mathcal B\) is the spherical Tits building for
\[
GL_4(\mathbb F_3).
\]

The Solomon--Tits theorem states that the building of proper nonzero
subspaces of an \(n\)-dimensional vector space over \(\mathbb F_q\) is
homotopy equivalent to a wedge of \((n-2)\)-spheres.

Thus in the present case
\[
|N(\mathcal B)|
\simeq
\bigvee S^2.
\]

Moreover, the top reduced homology is the Steinberg module. Its rank is
\[
q^{\binom n2}.
\]
For \(q=3\) and \(n=4\),
\[
\operatorname{rank}
\widetilde H_2(|N(\mathcal B)|;\mathbb Z)
=
3^{\binom42}
=
3^6
=
729.
