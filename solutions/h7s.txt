Answer: 2

Explanation:
Passing to the infinity-groupoid completion means formally inverting every morphism of C.

Choose the arrows

f_1 : 0 -> 1
and
g_1 : 1 -> 2

as a spanning tree. After groupoid completion, these tree arrows can be used to identify all three objects with the base object 0 without contributing generators to the fundamental group.

The remaining arrows initially give three loop generators:

one from f_2 relative to f_1,
one from f_3 relative to f_1,
and one from g_2 relative to g_1.

The defining relation

g_1 f_1 = g_2 f_2

becomes one relation among these three loop generators. Because all arrows are invertible in the groupoid completion, it allows the generator coming from g_2 to be expressed in terms of the generator coming from f_2.

Thus two free generators remain, with no further relations.

Hence

pi_1(C^gpd,0)

is a free group of rank 2.

Final answer: 2
