Explanation:
Under straightening/unstraightening, left fibrations over C correspond to functors

C -> S.

The left fibration L_F corresponds to F, while L_1 corresponds to the covariant representable

Map_C(1,-).

Therefore the mapping space of left fibrations is identified with the mapping space of natural transformations

Map_{Fun(C,S)}(Map_C(1,-), F).

By the covariant Yoneda lemma, this mapping space is equivalent to F(1).

Here F(1) is the discrete space {1,...,7}. Hence the mapping space has exactly 7 connected components.

Final answer: 7
