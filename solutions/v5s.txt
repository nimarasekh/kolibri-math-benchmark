For functors \(F,G:C\to\mathcal D\), the mapping object in a functor
\(\infty\)-category is given by the twisted-arrow end formula
\[
\operatorname{Map}_{\operatorname{Fun}(C,\mathcal D)}(F,G)
\simeq
\lim_{\operatorname{Tw}(C)}
\operatorname{Map}_{\mathcal D}(F(s),G(t)).
\]

Here both diagrams are constant, so the diagram of mapping spectra over
\(\operatorname{Tw}(\mathcal C)\) is constant with value
\[
E=
\operatorname{Map}_{H\mathbb Z}
\bigl(H(\mathbb Z/12),\Sigma^2H(\mathbb Z/18)\bigr).
\]
Thus the mapping spectrum is the cotensor of \(E\) by
\(|N\operatorname{Tw}(\mathcal C)|\).

The nerve of the twisted-arrow category is the edgewise subdivision of
the original nerve, so it has the same realization. The category
\(\mathcal I\) has nerve the square \(K_{2,2}\), hence
\[
|N(\mathcal I)|\simeq S^1.
\]
Therefore
\[
|N(\mathcal C)|
\simeq
|N(\mathcal I)|\times|N(\mathcal I)|
\simeq
T^2.
\]

Stably,
\[
\Sigma^\infty_+T^2
\simeq
S^0\vee S^1\vee S^1\vee S^2.
\]
Consequently the desired group of components has contributions from
\(\pi_0E\), two copies of \(\pi_1E\), and \(\pi_2E\).

Over \(\mathbb Z\),
\[
\pi_kE
\cong
\operatorname{Ext}^{2-k}_{\mathbb Z}
(\mathbb Z/12,\mathbb Z/18).
\]
Since \(\mathbb Z\) has global dimension \(1\),
\[
\pi_0E=\operatorname{Ext}^2=0,
\]
while
\[
\pi_1E
\cong
\operatorname{Ext}^1(\mathbb Z/12,\mathbb Z/18)
\cong
\mathbb Z/6,
\]
and
\[
\pi_2E
\cong
\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb Z/12,\mathbb Z/18)
\cong
\mathbb Z/6.
\]

Hence
\[
\pi_0
\operatorname{Map}_{\operatorname{Fun}(N(\mathcal C),\operatorname{Mod}_{H\mathbb Z})}
(F,G)
\cong
(\mathbb Z/6)^3,
\]
whose cardinality is
\[
6^3=216.
