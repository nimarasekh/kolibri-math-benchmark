Answer: 3024

Explanation:
Because X,Y,U,V are discrete spaces, the mapping space

Map_{Fun(Delta^1,S)}(f,g)

is itself discrete. Its points are exactly commutative squares

X -> U
|    |
f    g
v    v
Y -> V.

Choose the lower map b:Y->V first. Once b is fixed, an element x in the fiber f^{-1}(y) must be sent to an element of the fiber g^{-1}(b(y)).

If the fiber of f over y has size n and the chosen fiber of g has size m, then there are

m^n

possible choices over y.

The three fiber sizes of f are 1,2,3, and the available fiber sizes of g are also 1,2,3. Since the value b(y) can be chosen independently for each y, the total number of commutative squares is

(1^1 + 2^1 + 3^1)
(1^2 + 2^2 + 3^2)
(1^3 + 2^3 + 3^3).

These factors are

6, 14, 36.

Hence the total number of points, and therefore connected components, is

6 * 14 * 36 = 3024.

Final answer: 3024
