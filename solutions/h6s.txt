Answer: 60

Explanation:
For the poset [4] = {0<1<2<3<4}, the objects of the twisted-arrow category Tw([4]) are the arrows

i -> j

with i <= j.

We may identify such an object with the interval [i,j]. A morphism

(i,j) -> (i',j')

in Tw([4]) exists exactly when

i' <= i <= j <= j'.

Thus Tw([4]) is the poset of intervals in [4], ordered by inclusion.

A nondegenerate 3-simplex of its nerve is therefore a strict chain of four intervals

I_0 < I_1 < I_2 < I_3.

Starting from an interval [i,j], every strict enlargement is obtained by moving the left endpoint weakly to the left and/or the right endpoint weakly to the right, with at least one endpoint changing at each step.

Counting all strict chains of four intervals in the interval poset gives 60.

Equivalently, one can sum over the possible terminal interval [i,j] and count length-three strict chains in its lower interval; the total is again 60.

Final answer: 60
