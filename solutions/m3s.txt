Answer: 480

Explanation:
A nondegenerate 4-simplex of N(B_5) is a strict chain of five subsets

S_0 < S_1 < S_2 < S_3 < S_4.

For each of the five elements of {1,2,3,4,5}, record when it enters the chain:
it may already lie in S_0, enter at one of the four strict transitions, or never enter.
Thus there are six possible labels for each element.

For the chain to be strict, each of the four transition labels must occur at least once.
By inclusion-exclusion the number is

6^5 - C(4,1)5^5 + C(4,2)4^5 - C(4,3)3^5 + C(4,4)2^5
= 480.

Final answer: 480
