Since 3 is terminal in D, the pointwise left Kan extension at 3 is the colimit of F over C. Thus

(Lan_j F)(3) = F(1) amalgamated_{F(0)} F(2).

The six elements of F(0) impose the identifications

b1~c1, b1~c2,
b2~c2,
b3~c3, b3~c4,
b4~c4.

Therefore the elements of F(1) disjoint_union F(2) fall into the three equivalence classes

{b1,b2,c1,c2},
{b3,b4,c3,c4},
{c5}.

Hence the pushout has 3 elements.

Final answer: 3
