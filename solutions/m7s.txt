Answer: 7

Explanation:
The connected components of the homotopy pullback

BH x^h_{BG} BK

are indexed by the double cosets

H\G/K.

Here |G/K| = 24/2 = 12. The group H of order 2 acts on these 12 right cosets.

By Burnside's lemma, the number of H-orbits is

(12 + number_of_fixed_cosets)/2.

A coset gK is fixed by the nonidentity element (12) precisely when

g^{-1}(12)g lies in K.

The nonidentity element of K is (34). There are 4 elements g conjugating (12) to (34), and dividing by |K|=2 gives 2 fixed cosets.

Hence

(12+2)/2 = 7.

Final answer: 7
