Since \(t\) is the adjoined terminal object, the pointwise formula for
left Kan extensions gives
\[
(\operatorname{Lan}_jF)(t)
\simeq
\operatorname*{hocolim}_{C}F.
\]
For the span
\[
b\longleftarrow a\longrightarrow c,
\]
this homotopy colimit is the homotopy pushout
\[
T^2\amalg^h_{T^2}T^2.
\]

All three tori are connected. The Mayer--Vietoris sequence therefore
shows that
\[
H_1(X;\mathbb Z)
\cong
\operatorname{coker}
\left(
H_1(T^2)
\longrightarrow
H_1(T^2)\oplus H_1(T^2)
\right),
\]
where the map is represented, up to an irrelevant sign on one summand,
by
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
M\\
-N
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&2\\
6&0\\
0&-2\\
-6&-2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The Smith normal form of this \(4\times2\) matrix is
\[
\operatorname{diag}(2,6).
\]
Thus
\[
H_1(X;\mathbb Z)
\cong
\mathbb Z^2\oplus\mathbb Z/2\oplus\mathbb Z/6.
\]

The torsion subgroup therefore has order
\[
2\cdot6=12.
