Answer: 216

Explanation:
For objects A and B concentrated in degree 0, the connected components of

Map_{D(Z)}(A,B[1])

are

Hom_{D(Z)}(A,B[1])
~= Ext^1_Z(A,B).

Ext^1 is additive in each variable for finite direct sums, so

Ext^1_Z(
  Z/12 direct_sum Z/18,
  Z/8 direct_sum Z/27
)

is the direct sum of the four groups

Ext^1(Z/12,Z/8),
Ext^1(Z/12,Z/27),
Ext^1(Z/18,Z/8),
Ext^1(Z/18,Z/27).

For positive integers m,n,

Ext^1_Z(Z/m,Z/n) ~= Z/gcd(m,n).

Thus the four groups have orders

gcd(12,8)  = 4,
gcd(12,27) = 3,
gcd(18,8)  = 2,
gcd(18,27) = 9.

The direct sum therefore has

4 * 3 * 2 * 9 = 216

elements.

Hence the mapping space has 216 connected components.

Final answer: 216
