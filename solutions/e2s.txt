Explanation:
A natural transformation F => G consists of choices
eta_0(*) in G(0) and eta_1(*) in G(1)
such that the naturality square commutes.

Once eta_0(*) is chosen, eta_1(*) is forced to be its image under the structure map G(0) -> G(1).

Since G(0) has 3 elements, there are exactly 3 natural transformations.
