Answer: 35

Because the base is \(B\mathbb Z^2\), the functor
\[
F:B\mathbb Z^2\to\Cat_\infty
\]
describes an action of \(\mathbb Z^2\) on \(B\mathbb Z^4\). The
Grothendieck construction is therefore the action groupoid, equivalently
\[
\mathcal E\simeq B(\mathbb Z^4\rtimes\mathbb Z^2).
\]
In particular \(\mathcal E\) is already an \(\infty\)-groupoid, so its
groupoid completion changes nothing.

Hence
\[
H_1(\mathcal E^{\mathrm{gpd}};\mathbb Z)
\cong
(\mathbb Z^4\rtimes\mathbb Z^2)_{\mathrm{ab}}.
\]

For a semidirect product with abelian normal subgroup, the abelianization
is
\[
\mathbb Z^2\oplus
\frac{\mathbb Z^4}
{(M-I)\mathbb Z^4+(N-I)\mathbb Z^4}.
\]
The free summand \(\mathbb Z^2\) contributes no torsion, so we only need
the torsion of the coinvariant group.

The map whose image imposes the coinvariant relations is represented by
the \(4\times8\) integer matrix
\[
\left[\;M-I\mid N-I\;\right].
\]
Its Smith normal form is
\[
\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1,35).
\]

Consequently
\[
\frac{\mathbb Z^4}
{(M-I)\mathbb Z^4+(N-I)\mathbb Z^4}
\cong
\mathbb Z/35.
\]

Therefore the torsion subgroup of
\[
H_1(\mathcal E^{\mathrm{gpd}};\mathbb Z)
\]
has order \(35\).
