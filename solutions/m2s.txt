Answer: 16

Explanation:
The coend is the quotient of

(F(0) x G(0)) disjoint_union (F(1) x G(1))

by the relations

(F(alpha)(u),i) ~ (u,G(alpha)(i))

for the unique nonidentity arrow alpha:0->1, with u in F(1) and i in G(0).

There are initially

4*3 + 3*4 = 24

elements.

Using u->a, v->b, w->b and 1,2->x, 3->y, the relations produce four nontrivial equivalence classes:
{(a,1),(a,2),(u,x)},
{(a,3),(u,y)},
{(b,1),(b,2),(v,x),(w,x)},
{(b,3),(v,y),(w,y)}.

The remaining 12 elements are unaffected by the relations. Hence the quotient has

4 + 12 = 16

elements.

Final answer: 16
