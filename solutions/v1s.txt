The left fibration \(L_F\to N(C)\) is classified, under straightening, by
the functor
\[
F:C\to\mathcal S.
\]
The space of sections of \(L_F\) is therefore the homotopy limit
\[
\Gamma(N(C),L_F)\simeq \operatorname*{holim}_{C} F.
\]

Although \(C\) was presented using the monoid \(\mathbb N^2\), both
generators act on \(K(A,3)\) by equivalences. Thus the action factors,
for purposes of the homotopy limit, through the group completion
\[
\mathbb N^2\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^2,
\]
and the section space is the homotopy-fixed-point space
\[
K(A,3)^{h\mathbb Z^2}.
\]

The homotopy-fixed-point spectral sequence has
\[
E_2^{s,t}=H^s(\mathbb Z^2;\pi_tK(A,3)).
\]
Since the only nonzero positive homotopy group of \(K(A,3)\) is
\[
\pi_3K(A,3)=A,
\]
and \(\mathbb Z^2\) has cohomological dimension \(2\), we get
\[
\pi_1\Gamma(N(C),L_F)\cong H^2(\mathbb Z^2;A).
\]

For a \(\mathbb Z^2\)-module \(A\) with commuting actions \(\phi,\psi\),
the top cohomology is
\[
H^2(\mathbb Z^2;A)
\cong
A/\bigl((\phi-1)A+(\psi-1)A\bigr).
\]

Here \(A=(\mathbb Z/24)^4\), and
\[
(\phi-1)(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)=(4x_2,0,3x_4,0),
\]
\[
(\psi-1)(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)=(6x_2,0,8x_4,0).
\]

Thus the first coordinate is quotiented by the subgroup generated by
\(4\) and \(6\), leaving a group of order
\[
\gcd(24,4,6)=2.
\]
The third coordinate is quotiented by the subgroup generated by
\(3\) and \(8\), which is all of \(\mathbb Z/24\), since
\[
\gcd(24,3,8)=1.
\]
The second and fourth coordinates survive unchanged.

Therefore
\[
H^2(\mathbb Z^2;A)
\cong
\mathbb Z/2\oplus\mathbb Z/24\oplus\mathbb Z/24,
\]
whose order is
\[
2\cdot24\cdot24=1152.
