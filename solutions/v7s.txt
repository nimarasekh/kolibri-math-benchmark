The Barratt--Priddy--Quillen theorem identifies the group completion of
the symmetric monoidal \(E_\infty\)-space
\[
\coprod_{n\ge0}B\Sigma_n
\]
with the infinite loop space of the sphere spectrum:
\[
X^{\mathrm{gp}}
\simeq
QS^0
=
\Omega^\infty\mathbb S.
\]

The connected components of \(QS^0\) are indexed by
\[
\pi_0(QS^0)\cong\mathbb Z.
\]
Translation by the \(E_\infty\)-group structure identifies all
components, so in positive degrees the homotopy groups of the unit
component are the stable homotopy groups of spheres:
\[
\pi_7((X^{\mathrm{gp}})_0)
\cong
\pi_7^S.
\]

The seventh stable stem is the classical group
\[
\pi_7^S\cong\mathbb Z/240.
\]

Therefore
\[
\left|\pi_7((X^{\mathrm{gp}})_0)\right|=240.
