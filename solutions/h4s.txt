Answer: 2

Explanation:
Let X be the homotopy pushout of

S^1 <-^4 S^1 ->^6 S^1.

The Mayer-Vietoris sequence for this homotopy pushout contains

H_1(S^1;Z) -> H_1(S^1;Z) direct_sum H_1(S^1;Z) -> H_1(X;Z) -> H_0(S^1;Z).

All three circles are connected, so the map on H_0 from the middle circle to the two outer circles is injective. Hence

H_1(X;Z)

is the cokernel of the map on H_1.

Identifying each H_1(S^1;Z) with Z, this map is

Z -> Z^2,
n -> (4n,-6n).

Therefore

H_1(X;Z) ~= Z^2 / <(4,-6)>.

By Smith normal form, a quotient of Z^2 by the subgroup generated by (4,-6) is

Z direct_sum Z/gcd(4,6).

Since gcd(4,6)=2,

H_1(X;Z) ~= Z direct_sum Z/2.

The torsion subgroup therefore has order 2.

Final answer: 2
