Answer: 36

Write
\[
A=\mathbb Z/12\oplus\mathbb Z/20,\qquad
B=\mathbb Z/18\oplus\mathbb Z/30,\qquad
D=\mathbb Z/24.
\]
The cofiber sequence
\[
HA\longrightarrow HB\longrightarrow C\longrightarrow\Sigma HA
\]
gives, after mapping into \(\Sigma HD\), an exact sequence
\[
\operatorname{Hom}(B,D)
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Hom}(A,D)
\longrightarrow
[C,\Sigma HD]
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Ext}^1(B,D)
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Ext}^1(A,D).
\]

Thus the order of
\[
[C,\Sigma HD]
=
\pi_0\operatorname{Map}(C,\Sigma HD)
\]
is the product of

1. the order of the cokernel of
\[
\operatorname{Hom}(B,D)\to\operatorname{Hom}(A,D),
\]
and

2. the order of the kernel of
\[
\operatorname{Ext}^1(B,D)\to\operatorname{Ext}^1(A,D).
\]

For the first summand, precomposition with
\[
\mathbb Z/12\to\mathbb Z/18,\qquad x\mapsto3x
\]
gives a Hom-map whose cokernel has order \(6\).

For the second summand,
\[
\mathbb Z/20\to\mathbb Z/30,\qquad y\mapsto3y,
\]
the corresponding Hom-map has cokernel of order \(2\).

Hence the total Hom-cokernel has order
\[
6\cdot2=12.
\]

For Ext, use
\[
\operatorname{Ext}^1(\mathbb Z/n,\mathbb Z/24)
\cong
\mathbb Z/\gcd(n,24).
\]
The first component gives
\[
\mathbb Z/6\longrightarrow\mathbb Z/12,
\qquad x\mapsto2x,
\]
which has trivial kernel.

The second gives
\[
\mathbb Z/6\longrightarrow\mathbb Z/4,
\qquad x\mapsto2x,
\]
whose kernel has \(3\) elements.

Therefore the Ext-kernel has order \(3\), and the required mapping group
has order
\[
12\cdot3=36.
